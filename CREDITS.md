# Credits and asset provenance

Soliton Portable is an unofficial fan application. Metal Gear Solid and its original audio are owned by Konami and their respective rightsholders.

Short MGS1 effects were obtained from the public Myinstants clips referenced by Daniel Herzog's [Metal Gear Solid soundboard](https://github.com/wikiti/metal-gear-solid-soundboard). That reference was used as an asset index; its application code was not copied. Exact URLs and SHA256 hashes are in `assets/sounds/provenance.json`.

Menu clips:

- [MGS1 Menu Select](https://www.myinstants.com/en/instant/mgs1-menu-select-76120/)
- [MGS1 Menu Move](https://www.myinstants.com/pt/instant/mgs1-menu-move-71733/)
- MGS1 Menu Open, `https://www.myinstants.com/media/sounds/mgs1-menu-open.mp3`

The MGS1-style HUD, Codec and Ammo fonts are Andrew Gleeson's [MGS1 fonts](https://gleeson.itch.io/mgs1-fonts), distributed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Unmodified copies were obtained from the public [Roomshift font assets](https://github.com/Jenil005/roomshift/tree/913ef5017f831e68e1d98ba43f0a6794d7d279d5/frontend/src/assets/fonts). Font hashes are recorded in `assets/visual-provenance.json`.

RATION, SOCOM and the GAME OVER art are original MGS1 imagery reused from the companion personal device theme. Campbell and Snake portraits were obtained from the [FoxhoundAI codec assets](https://github.com/SlickAmogus/FoxhoundAI/tree/master/SnakeAI/Content/codex). The radar surface texture is a crop of an MGS1 radar reference. The [official MGS1 manual](https://metalgear.konami.net/manual/mc1/mgs1/pc/en/page08.html) was used as a visual reference.

The short incoming CODEC sound comes from the same soundboard index, `https://www.myinstants.com/media/sounds/codec.mp3`. The connection sound is [codecopen.wav from FoxhoundAI](https://github.com/SlickAmogus/FoxhoundAI/blob/77a28d3dbb1d7557ca5619132bfe4d669694b76f/SnakeAI/Content/codecopen.wav). Snake and Campbell's opening exchange comes from the [recorded PS1 dock call](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WqYfDjbm8-s&t=12s). Campbell's infiltration order comes from the [heliport CODEC call at 6:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WqYfDjbm8-s&t=381s).

The in-game Cargo Dock cue comes from [Metal Gear Solid Integral, disc 1, track 5](https://downloads.khinsider.com/game-soundtracks/album/metal-gear-solid-integral-ps1-ps4-ps5-switch-xbox-one-xbox-series-xs-gamerip-1999). ALERT uses Encounter and lets it fade during EVASION from the [PS1 game recordings](https://downloads.khinsider.com/game-soundtracks/album/metal-gear-solid-ps1-gamerip-1998). These local excerpts are encoded for looping. Music and game sounds belong to their respective rightsholders. Guard rifle fire and the guard's noise investigation line are indexed alongside the other effects. The suppressed SOCOM HUD sprite adapts the existing game image.
